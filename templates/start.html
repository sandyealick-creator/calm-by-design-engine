This check-in is for approved Calm by Design participants who have already completed Sandy’s outside membership and participation process.

Calm by Design is educational wellness support. It is not medical care, therapy, diagnosis, crisis care, or emergency monitoring.

You may be asked about how you are feeling, including physical symptoms, anxiety, energy, sleep, and what is on your mind. Your responses may be processed through the approved Calm by Design support systems to suggest a grounding practice or next step.

Important safety reminder

This app is not monitored in real time. If you may be in immediate danger, may hurt yourself, cannot stay safe, or may be having a medical emergency, call 911 or contact emergency services now. In the United States, you may also call or text 988 for mental health crisis support. Do not wait for this app or a reply from White Raven Holistic.
